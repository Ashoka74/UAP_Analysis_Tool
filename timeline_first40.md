**1940** — From 1940 to 1945, Allied and German aviators in multiple theaters reported swarms of red or green luminous balls and small metal-looking disks that followed aircraft, circled them, and at times appeared to touch wings or tail assemblies without damage. The objects were usually not seen on then-current radar and were first called "Kraut fireballs" and later "foo fighters." They became more common during the first major daylight bombing raids over Germany and were also observed from the ground; wartime authorities initially suspected a secret German weapon, while German pilots reportedly suspected a secret U.S. weapon. The report notes that secrecy may have suppressed files until 1949 and mentions other larger cigar-, disk-, and sphere-shaped sightings in both camps. ([255_413270_UFO's_and_Defense_What_Should_we_Prepar, pp. 75–76](<https://www.war.gov/medialink/ufo/release_1/255_413270_ufo's_and_defense_what_should_we_prepare_for.pdf>))
